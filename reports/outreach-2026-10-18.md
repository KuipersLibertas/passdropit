# Cold Outreach Template — 2026-10-18

**Target:** YouTube creators in the video editing / LUT / Lightroom preset niche who share free downloads via Dropbox links in their video descriptions.

**Angle:** Their Dropbox links never expire. Subscribers from 2 years ago can still download "limited" freebies. Show them how to fix it.

**Channel:** YouTube community comment, Twitter DM, or email (if listed in About tab).

---

## Template

Subject (email): Your free download links are still live — worth knowing

Hey [Name],

Watched your [video title] recently — the free LUT pack you linked is still in the description and still downloading.

Not a problem if you intended that, but if you ever want to make a freebie time-limited (e.g. "available this week only"), Dropbox doesn't let you set a link expiry on a free account. You'd need to manually delete the link when you're done.

There's a tool called Passdropit that wraps your existing Dropbox link and lets you set an automatic expiry date or a download limit — no need to change your Dropbox plan. Thought it might be useful given how often you share files with your audience.

Worth a look: passdropit.com

[Your name]
