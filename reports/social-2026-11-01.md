## LinkedIn

If you give away digital files to grow an audience (presets, templates, sample packs), you're probably flying blind on one basic question: how many people actually downloaded the thing?

Dropbox doesn't tell you. Its old Traffic & Insights feature was retired in 2021, and anyone who opens a public shared link appears as an anonymous guest. On the free plan, you get no insight at all.

That's a real gap for creators. Download numbers tell you which content converts, where your audience is, and whether a premium version would sell.

The fix I recommend: put a tracking layer in front of the link you already have. With Passdropit, your files stay in Dropbox or Google Drive. You share a passdropit.com link instead, and every access is counted, with location data on Pro. Add a password you only mention in the video, and the count reflects your real audience instead of whoever found the link in a forum.
