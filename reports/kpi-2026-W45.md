## Anomalies / Flags
- **Users −31%, sessions −27%: mostly not a real decline.** Two causes:
  1. **Bot traffic stopped.** US active users fell from 808 to 265 (−67%). GA4 says Boardman (AWS Oregon) spam traffic fell 86% week on week. W44's US count was inflated by roughly 680 bot "users"; take those out and W44 had about 1,320 real users, close to W45's 1,380.
  2. **Partial Sunday.** Nov 8 is only partly counted.
- **Direct sessions halved (1,164 → 590, −49%).** This is the same bot effect. Bot sessions show up as Direct, so the Direct drop is mostly the Boardman drop.
- **Form starts went up while traffic went down (+2%).** Form starts per user rose from 0.46 to 0.69. The remaining traffic is better quality, which also points to W44 being padded by bots.
- **Ad revenue down about 45%.** W44 included an unusual €0.30 day (Nov 1, eCPM €1.09). Without that day W44 was about €0.55, in line with the "sustainable €0.55–0.65/wk" estimate. W45 eCPM fell every day, from €0.34 to €0.14 by Nov 7, while paid impressions held at about 240–290 a day. **eCPM is falling, not traffic.** Worth a Revenue Agent look.
- **File download tracking is still broken.** GA4 counted 28 `file_download` events against an expected ~2.5K downloads a week from the DB baseline. GA4 cannot measure downloads; we need DB access.
- **Organic Search is still collapsed:** 7 sessions, under 0.5%, for the fourth week running (W42 5 → W44 5 → W45 7).
- **Stripe: past-due subscriptions are still unresolved.** Both $5 subscriptions from the "HUSTLE" customer (created 28 Mar 2026) are still *Achterstallig* (past due). Only the $9 subscription (shop@lovelylittlestock, since Jan 2023) is collecting. **Revenue actually being collected is $9/mo**, not $19.
