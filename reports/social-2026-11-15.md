## LinkedIn

Producers who give away sample packs and drum kits all run into the same problem: the link leaks.

A public Dropbox or Google Drive link works for anyone who has it, and it never expires. Within days it gets reposted in Discord servers and on "free kits" blogs. Google Drive shows no download count for a public link, and Dropbox retired its Traffic & Insights feature in 2021. Most producers find out about a leak from a comment, not from their own data.

The fixes people usually try don't hold up. "Please don't share" in the readme gets ignored. A password on the zip leaks along with the zip. Making the folder private locks out your real audience too.

What works better is gating the link itself:

• Put a password in front of the cloud link, and say it in the video, not the description.
• Give each giveaway a lifespan, either a date or a number of downloads.
• Track downloads per link, so a sudden spike from an unfamiliar country tells you the link has been posted somewhere.
• Keep the full version one step away, behind your email list or store.

The pack never leaves Dropbox or Google Drive. Only the link in front of it changes.
