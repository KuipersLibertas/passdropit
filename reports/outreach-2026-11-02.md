# Cold Outreach Template — 2026-11-02

**Draft only — nothing has been sent.** Per `reports/growth-2026-11.md` (Addendum, Run 2), any creator outreach for this niche needs explicit user approval first.

**Target:** Alight Motion / CapCut edit-pack creators (mostly India, Indonesia, Philippines) who share XML presets, CC packs or "subscriber special" packs through public Google Drive links in their YouTube descriptions.

**Angle:** Help, not pitch. Their Drive links get reshared to Telegram and preset-dump sites, they can't see download counts, and viewers grab the link without watching. A password said in the video fixes all three without moving their files.

**Channel:** Email from the channel's About tab, or Instagram DM (most of these creators list one). Avoid YouTube comments for anything that looks like a pitch.

**Supporting links:** `/blog/how-to-share-alight-motion-presets-with-a-password`, `/blog/subscriber-special-preset-pack-password-in-video`

**Notes for personalization:**
- Name a real video and a real effect from it. Generic praise reads as spam.
- If they already use a password or a link locker, don't send this.
- Don't mention Pro or pricing. The free tier covers the password and download count.
- DM version: drop the subject and the last line, keep it to 5 sentences.
- Don't claim their pack *has* leaked unless you've actually seen it on a reupload site. Use "often end up" otherwise, and adjust the subject line.
- Many of these creators are in India and Indonesia. A Hindi or Bahasa Indonesia version may land better — have a native speaker check it before use.
