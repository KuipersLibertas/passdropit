## Niches evaluated

### 1. Music production — sample packs, drum kits, presets (RECOMMENDED)
- **Community size:** Very large, English-dominant (US/UK/CA/AU heavy). Many free-pack roundups exist (EDMProd, HipHopMakers, LANDR 2026 list) — evidence of demand. Size estimate: tens of thousands of active free-pack publishers; exact numbers unverified.
- **How they share files:** Dropbox / Google Drive / MEGA links in video descriptions (a 400-sounds pack on Dropbox was found on itch.io; "FREE STARTER PACK" and free-pack-playlist YouTube videos use description links), plus Gumroad/itch.io/Looperman/SoundCloud.
- **Pain points:** Packs get re-uploaded to piracy/"free VST" sites (freevstplugins.it.com republishes dropgun/dabro packs); no idea who downloaded; no way to cap downloads or gate behind an email/password for Patreon supporters; links stay live forever.
- **Sample videos found (channel names/subs unverified):**
  - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FFaGLX3g8K0 — "FREE STARTER PACK FOR MUSIC PRODUCERS"
  - https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLOUt1iy70AGUwvYV3umj-KCmRsze5ni63 — "Free pack Sample (All music genre)"
  - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ycx_woA4cpg — "5 Sample Packs Every Producer Needs In 2024"
- **Fit:** Password + expiry for supporter-only drops, download caps, IP tracking vs. piracy re-sharing. Strong.

### 2. Procreate / digital art brushes
- **Community size:** Large, English-leaning but with big non-Tier-1 share (Philippines, Brazil, Indonesia, Europe). Procreate Folio forum is an active hub.
- **How they share:** Google Drive folders (Procreate Folio posts, a 21-brush pack via Drive+Discord), MEGA, Gumroad "free" listings. Drive's "open in" import quirk is a known friction point.
- **Pain points:** Drive link permission errors, no tracking, paid packs leaked via public Drive links.
- **Sample YouTube videos (channels unverified):** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YZDyFHVi7Dg ("My Procreate Brushes Set - Free Download", Drive + MEGA), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ehFNr9YyCRA, https://www.youtube.com/shorts/o4ckTCkK0Kc.
- **Fit:** Good, but audience skews hobbyist/free-seeking; Pro conversion likely lower and Tier 1 share more diluted than music.

### 3. Notion templates
- **Community size:** Large, English-dominant.
- **How they share:** Almost entirely Gumroad/Payhip/Notion "duplicate" links — not Dropbox/Drive. Search returned no YouTube-with-Drive evidence.
- **Pain points:** Duplicate links are public and re-sellable; however Notion's native share model is the distribution mechanism.
- **Fit:** Weak for now (they don't use file hosts). Revisit only if gating Notion public links is a product feature (one directory claims Passdropit supports Notion links — verify).

## Recommendation: Music production (sample packs / drum kits)
Highest Tier 1 concentration, clearest Dropbox/Drive-in-description behavior, a concrete pain (piracy re-uploads, supporter-only drops), and producers routinely run Patreon/email-gated "free pack" funnels where a password or limit is natural. Easiest outreach: small producers list public emails in channel "About" pages.
