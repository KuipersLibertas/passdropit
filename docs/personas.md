## The Lightroom Preset Creator — [Historical Core Audience]
**Platform:** YouTube (Southeast Asian market — Indonesia, Philippines)
**File type shared:** Lightroom presets (.dng, .xmp files)
**Current sharing method:** Dropbox public links in video descriptions
**Pain point:** No visibility into who downloads, links get reshared beyond intended audience
**Passdropit fit:** Password + IP tracking + download count
**Acquisition channel:** YouTube organic video (still active but community migrating)
**Status:** Existing base — partially churned to other tools in 2024

## The Beatmaker / Sample Pack Producer — Music Production (Tier 1 target)
**Status:** Hypothesis (2026-11) — not yet validated with real creators
**Platform:** YouTube, Instagram, SoundCloud; sells via Gumroad/itch.io/Patreon
**File type shared:** Sample packs, drum kits, MIDI packs, synth presets (large .zip/.wav)
**Current sharing method:** Dropbox / Google Drive / MEGA links in video descriptions
**Pain point:** Packs re-uploaded to piracy "free VST" sites; no download visibility; links never expire; supporter-only drops can't be gated
**Passdropit fit:** Password, download limit, expiry, IP tracking
**Acquisition channel:** Blog SEO (producer-protection guides), user-approved personal outreach, producer communities
**Estimated TAM:** Large, English-dominant; exact size unverified

## The Digital Artist — Procreate Brush Maker (secondary)
**Status:** Hypothesis (2026-11)
**Platform:** YouTube, X, Procreate Folio, Gumroad
