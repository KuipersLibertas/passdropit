# Passdropit — Brand & Audience Context

## Brand Voice
- **Practical and direct** — creators want tools that work, not marketing fluff
- **Creator-first** — speak to the person sharing files, not a corporate IT buyer
- **Simple** — the product is simple; the copy should be too
- **Trustworthy** — privacy and control are the core value proposition

## Tone Guidelines
- DO: Use concrete examples ("when you share a Lightroom preset pack", "your sample pack download page")
- DO: Short sentences. Active voice. Get to the point.
- DON'T: "In today's digital world...", "Leverage synergies...", "Seamlessly..."
- DON'T: Oversell. One clear benefit per sentence.

## Target Audiences

### 1. YouTube Content Creators — Primary
Creators who produce tutorials, showcases, or educational content and share downloadable files in their video descriptions or community posts.

Sub-niches (current and potential):
- **Photography presets** (Lightroom .dng, .xmp) — *was* the core audience (Southeast Asian photographers), now migrating
- **Music production** (drum kits, sample packs, presets, DAW templates)
- **Video editing** (LUTs, After Effects templates, Premiere presets, sound effects)
- **Graphic design** (Procreate brushes, Photoshop actions, Canva templates)
- **Gaming** (mods, configs, overlays)
- **Education** (PDF course materials, worksheets, templates)

**Why they need Passdropit:** Dropbox/GDrive public links have no download tracking, no control over who accesses files, links can be shared beyond the intended audience.

### 2. Freelancers & Agencies — Secondary
Share client deliverables with controlled access and proof of delivery (IP tracking).

### 3. Stock Creators — Emerging
Music, video, and photo creators selling or distributing watermarked previews with download-gated full versions.

### 4. Educators — Emerging
Course creators and teachers distributing materials only to enrolled students.

## Core Value Props (by audience)
| Audience | Primary Pain | Passdropit Solves |
|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| YouTubers | No idea who downloads their files | IP tracking + download count |
| YouTubers | Links get reshared beyond fans | Password protection |
| Freelancers | No delivery confirmation | Email notification + IP log |
| Stock creators | Can't monetize file access | Pay-per-download feature |
| Educators | Files shared beyond students | Password + download limits |

## Competitor Landscape
- **WeTransfer** — upload-based (not link-gating), limited control
- **Files.fm** — more complex, subscription-heavy
- **Filepass** — similar but focuses on musicians
- **Direct Dropbox/GDrive** — free but zero gating or tracking
- **Passdropit's edge:** Fastest path from existing cloud storage link to gated link; no re-upload needed

## Content Tone Examples

**Good:**
> "Share your drum kit with your YouTube audience — and actually see who downloads it."

**Bad:**
> "Passdropit offers a comprehensive solution for content creators seeking enhanced digital asset management capabilities."

## Naming Conventions
- Product name: **Passdropit** (one word, lowercase 'd')
- Domain: passdropit.com
- Tagline (current): "Protect and share your Dropbox and Google Drive links"
